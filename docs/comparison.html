If you skip everything above, read this

  1. Under 24 GB of VRAM and want quality: LoRA. Almost always the right first bet.
  2. Under 12 GB and a big base model (7B+): QLoRA. Same tradeoffs, quantized.
  3. You need to align tone or preference, not learn new facts: DPO.
  4. You already have two fine-tunes and want a third for free: Model merging.
  5. You have unlimited compute and want the last one percent: Full fine-tuning.
  6. You are doing a controlled experiment on layer capacity: Linear probing.
Honest disclaimer No comparison table survives contact with a real dataset. Use these numbers to narrow the field, then run two candidates end-to-end. The winner is the one that actually works on your task.
